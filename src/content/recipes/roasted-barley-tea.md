Toast until it smells like popcorn. That’s the whole recipe.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup hulled barley
- 6 cups water
- Pinch salt

## Instructions

1. Toast barley in a dry pot until deep brown and fragrant.
2. Add water, simmer 15 minutes.
3. Strain. Drink hot or iced.
