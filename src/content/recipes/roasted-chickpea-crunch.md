They only stay crisp if you dry the chickpeas well before they hit the pan.

## Ingredients

- 1 can chickpeas, drained and patted dry
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Zest of 1 lemon

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss chickpeas with oil, chili, and salt.
2. Roast 30 minutes, shaking once, until they rattle on the sheet.
3. Toss with lemon zest while hot.
