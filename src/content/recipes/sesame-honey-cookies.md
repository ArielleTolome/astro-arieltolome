Pull them when the edges set and the middle looks wet.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup tahini
- 1/2 cup honey
- 1 egg
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- Sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Mix tahini, honey, egg, soda.
2. Scoop, roll in sesame.
3. Bake 350°F 10 minutes.
