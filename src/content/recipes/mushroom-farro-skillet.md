Brown the mushrooms hard before the farro goes in.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup farro
- 1 lb mixed mushrooms
- 1 onion
- Thyme
- 3 cups broth

## Instructions

1. Sear mushrooms in oil until they squeak. Set aside.
2. Toast farro with onion, add broth, simmer 25 minutes.
3. Fold mushrooms and thyme back in.
