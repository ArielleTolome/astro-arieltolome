The oil goes in after popping so it doesn’t scorch.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup popcorn kernels
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Black pepper
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Pop kernels in 1 tbsp oil with a lid.
2. Drizzle remaining oil.
3. Salt and a lot of pepper.
