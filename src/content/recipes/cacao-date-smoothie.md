Soak the dates if they’re stiff or you’ll get flecks.

## Ingredients

- 4 dates, pitted
- 1 tbsp cacao
- 1 cup almond milk
- 1 banana
- Pinch salt

## Instructions

1. Blend until completely smooth.
2. Pour. No garnish required.
