The salt is what makes it taste like fruit instead of candy.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups watermelon, cubed
- 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 1 tsp sea salt
- Soda water

## Instructions

1. Macerate watermelon with sugar and salt 15 minutes.
2. Stir in vinegar, strain.
3. Serve 1 part shrub to 3 parts soda.
