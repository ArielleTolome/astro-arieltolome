Use pears that are almost too soft. Hard ones never give up.

## Ingredients

- 4 pears, halved
- 1 vanilla bean or 1 tsp extract
- 2 tbsp sugar
- Butter

## Instructions

1. Dot pears with butter and sugar, vanilla.
2. Roast 375°F 30 minutes until collapsed.
3. Spoon the juices over.
