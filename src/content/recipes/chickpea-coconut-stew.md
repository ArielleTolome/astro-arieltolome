Let it reduce until the oil starts to separate. That’s when it tastes finished.

## Ingredients

- 2 cans chickpeas
- 1 can coconut milk
- 1 cup crushed tomato
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 onion

## Instructions

1. Soften onion in oil. Add turmeric.
2. Add tomato, coconut milk, chickpeas.
3. Simmer 20 minutes until thick.
