Sift the matcha or it clumps.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 avocado
- 1 tsp matcha
- 1 cup cold milk
- 1 tsp honey

## Instructions

1. Blend avocado, milk, honey.
2. Sift matcha on top and blend 5 more seconds.
