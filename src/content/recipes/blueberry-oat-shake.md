The oats make it drink like breakfast, not juice.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup frozen blueberries
- 1/4 cup oats
- 1 cup oat milk
- 1 tsp maple

## Instructions

1. Blend until the oats disappear.
2. Drink cold.
