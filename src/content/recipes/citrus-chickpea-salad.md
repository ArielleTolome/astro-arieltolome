This is lunch if you keep a can of chickpeas around. The orange juice is the dressing.

## Ingredients

- 1 can chickpeas, drained
- 1 orange, segmented (juice reserved)
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 cup parsley, chopped
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper

## Instructions

1. Soak onion in cold water 5 minutes, then drain.
2. Toss chickpeas with orange segments, onion, and parsley.
3. Dress with reserved juice, oil, salt, and pepper.
