The cauliflower should look almost burnt on the edges.

## Ingredients

- 1 head cauliflower
- 2 tsp cumin
- 8 tortillas
- Cabbage slaw
- Lime yogurt

## Instructions

1. Roast cauliflower with cumin and oil at 450°F for 25 minutes.
2. Warm tortillas.
3. Fill with cauliflower, slaw, crema.
