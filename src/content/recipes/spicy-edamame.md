Salt them while they steam so it sticks.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb frozen edamame in pods
- 1 tsp chili flakes
- Flaky salt
- Sesame oil

## Instructions

1. Steam or boil edamame 5 minutes.
2. Toss with chili, salt, sesame oil.
3. Serve in a bowl you can throw the pods into.
