Don’t blend this one. You want to see through it a little.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb tomatoes
- 1 fennel bulb
- 4 cups water
- Garlic
- Olive oil

## Instructions

1. Simmer tomatoes, garlic, water 25 minutes. Strain.
2. Add shaved fennel to the hot broth.
3. Oil and salt.
