Low heat. If it sticks, you rushed it.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup jasmine rice
- 1 can coconut milk
- 2 cups water
- 3 tbsp sugar
- Salt
- Lime zest

## Instructions

1. Simmer rice, coconut milk, water, sugar, salt 30 minutes, stirring.
2. It should be loose; it thickens off heat.
3. Lime zest.
