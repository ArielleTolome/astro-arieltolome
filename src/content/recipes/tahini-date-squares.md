Press the mix firmly or the squares crumble. Chill before you cut.

## Ingredients

- 12 Medjool dates, pitted
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 1/3 cup tahini
- 1/2 tsp vanilla
- Pinch of salt
- Sesame seeds, to finish

## Instructions

1. Pulse dates, oats, tahini, vanilla, and salt until the mix holds when squeezed.
2. Press into a small lined pan. Sprinkle sesame.
3. Chill 1 hour, then cut into squares.
