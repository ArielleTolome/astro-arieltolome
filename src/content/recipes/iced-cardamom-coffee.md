Crush the pods, don’t powder them, or it turns dusty.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup strong cold brew
- 4 cardamom pods, crushed
- 1/2 cup oat milk
- Ice
- 1 tsp maple

## Instructions

1. Shake cold brew, cardamom, maple, and ice 20 seconds.
2. Strain over fresh ice.
3. Top with oat milk.
