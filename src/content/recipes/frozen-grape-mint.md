Freeze the grapes ahead. That’s the whole trick.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups frozen grapes
- 8 mint leaves
- Splash of water
- Squeeze of lemon

## Instructions

1. Blend grapes and mint. Add water to catch the blades.
2. Lemon at the end.
