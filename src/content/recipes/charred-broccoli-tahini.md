If it still looks green and polite, it isn’t done.

## Ingredients

- 2 heads broccoli
- 3 tbsp tahini
- 1 lemon
- Garlic
- Oil

## Instructions

1. Roast broccoli at 450°F until charred, 20 minutes.
2. Whisk tahini, lemon, garlic, water until pourable.
3. Spoon sauce over broccoli.
