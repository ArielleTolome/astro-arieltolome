The salt is what makes it taste like mango, not dessert.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups mango
- 1 cup yogurt
- 1 lime
- Pinch salt
- Ice

## Instructions

1. Blend everything until smooth.
2. Taste for salt.
3. Serve immediately.
