Foil-roast the beets so they steam in their own juice.

## Ingredients

- 4 beets
- 1/2 cup walnuts
- 1 orange
- Olive oil
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Wrap beets, roast 40 minutes at 400°F. Peel.
2. Toast walnuts.
3. Slice beets, dress with orange juice and oil, walnuts on top.
