Use the bean liquid. That’s the sauce.

## Ingredients

- 2 cans cannellini, not drained
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 lemon
- Parsley
- Olive oil
- Chili flakes

## Instructions

1. Warm garlic in a lot of oil.
2. Add beans and their liquid, simmer 10 minutes.
3. Off heat: lemon zest, juice, parsley, chili.
