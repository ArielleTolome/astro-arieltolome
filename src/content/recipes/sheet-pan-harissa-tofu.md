Press the tofu or it won’t take color.

## Ingredients

- 1 block extra-firm tofu
- 2 tbsp harissa
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 red onion
- 1 zucchini

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss everything with harissa and oil.
2. Spread out. Roast 25 minutes, flipping once.
3. Salt at the end.
