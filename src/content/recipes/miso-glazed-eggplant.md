The glaze should blister, not burn. Watch the last two minutes.

## Ingredients

- 2 globe eggplants, halved
- 2 tbsp white miso
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tsp sugar
- Sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Score eggplant, brush with oil, roast 20 minutes at 425°F.
2. Mix miso, mirin, sugar. Brush on.
3. Broil 2 minutes. Sesame on top.
