Rinse the noodles cold or they glue together.

## Ingredients

- 8 oz soba
- 3 tbsp peanut butter
- 1 lime
- 1 tbsp soy
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- Cucumber

## Instructions

1. Boil soba, rinse under cold water.
2. Whisk peanut butter, lime, soy, sesame, splash of water.
3. Toss with noodles and cucumber.
