One chipotle is enough. Two if you mean it.

## Ingredients

- 2 cans black beans
- 1 chipotle in adobo
- 1 onion
- Lime
- Cumin

## Instructions

1. Cook onion, cumin, chipotle.
2. Add beans and water, simmer 15 minutes.
3. Blend half, lime at the end.
