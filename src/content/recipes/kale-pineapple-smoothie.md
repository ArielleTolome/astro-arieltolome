The pineapple covers the kale. Use frozen fruit so you skip the ice.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup frozen pineapple
- 1 handful kale, stems pulled
- 1 cup coconut water
- 1/2 banana
- Squeeze of lime

## Instructions

1. Blend everything until no kale flecks remain.
2. Taste for lime. Drink immediately.
