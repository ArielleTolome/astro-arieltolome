It looks ugly until the lemon goes in. Then it tastes finished.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup red lentils
- 1 onion
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 lemon
- 5 cups water

## Instructions

1. Cook onion, cumin, lentils, water 20 minutes.
2. Blend half.
3. Lemon juice, more than you think.
