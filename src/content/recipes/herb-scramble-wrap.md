Pull it off the heat while it still looks wet. It keeps cooking.

## Ingredients

- 8 oz firm tofu, crumbled
- 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
- Handful parsley and chives
- 2 tortillas
- Hot sauce

## Instructions

1. Cook tofu in a slick of oil with yeast and salt until just set.
2. Fold in herbs off heat.
3. Wrap in warm tortillas with hot sauce.
