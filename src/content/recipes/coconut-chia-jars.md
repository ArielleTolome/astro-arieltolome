Stir twice in the first ten minutes or you get a clump.

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup chia seeds
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 1 tbsp maple
- 1 mango, diced
- Pinch salt

## Instructions

1. Stir chia, coconut milk, maple, and salt.
2. Wait 10 minutes, stir again, chill overnight.
3. Top with mango.
