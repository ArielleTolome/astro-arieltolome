Don’t drain a thing. Starchy water is the cream.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup orzo
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes
- 1/2 cup olives
- 2 garlic cloves
- 2 cups broth

## Instructions

1. Sizzle garlic and tomatoes until they burst.
2. Add orzo, toast 1 minute, add broth.
3. Stir until creamy. Fold in olives.
