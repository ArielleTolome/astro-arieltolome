Tear, don’t cut. Ragged edges brown better.

## Ingredients

- 4 pitas
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp za'atar
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 400°F. Tear pita, toss with oil, za'atar, salt.
2. Bake 10 minutes until crisp.
