Keep stirring. The oats should slump, not sit in lumps.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rolled oats
- 4 cups water
- 1 inch ginger
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- Scallions
- Sesame oil

## Instructions

1. Simmer oats, water, and ginger 20 minutes, stirring.
2. Season with soy.
3. Finish with scallion and sesame oil.
