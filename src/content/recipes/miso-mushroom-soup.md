Boiling miso kills it. Stir it in a ladle of broth first.

## Ingredients

- 8 oz mushrooms
- 4 cups water
- 2 tbsp miso
- Tofu cubes
- Scallion

## Instructions

1. Simmer mushrooms in water 15 minutes.
2. Ladle broth into miso, then return.
3. Add tofu and scallion.
