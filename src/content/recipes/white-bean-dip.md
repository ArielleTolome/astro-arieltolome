Keep it thick. Thin dips taste like baby food.

## Ingredients

- 1 can white beans
- 1 lemon
- 1 garlic clove
- Olive oil
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Blend beans, garlic, lemon, salt, 2 tbsp oil.
2. Spoon into a bowl, well in the center, more oil.
