Pull them when they look wet. They crisp as they cool.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups pepitas
- 2 tbsp maple
- 1/2 tsp chili
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Toss pepitas with maple, chili, salt.
2. Toast in a skillet, stirring, 6 minutes.
3. Cool on a plate.
