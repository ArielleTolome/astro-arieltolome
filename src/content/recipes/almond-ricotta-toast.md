The pepper on honey is the whole point.

## Ingredients

- 2 thick slices sourdough
- 1/2 cup almond ricotta
- 1 tbsp honey
- Black pepper
- Olive oil

## Instructions

1. Toast the bread hard.
2. Spread ricotta, drizzle honey and oil.
3. Crack pepper over the top.
