Don’t crowd the pan or it steams. You want brown edges.

## Ingredients

- 2 sweet potatoes, diced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp oil
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Heat oil until it shimmers. Add potato in one layer.
2. Leave it 4 minutes before stirring.
3. Add onion and paprika, cook until both are browned.
