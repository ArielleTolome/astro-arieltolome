Brew the flowers strong. Lime keeps it from tasting like perfume.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup dried hibiscus
- 4 cups water
- 2 limes, juiced
- 2 tbsp sugar
- Ice

## Instructions

1. Simmer hibiscus in water 10 minutes. Strain and cool.
2. Stir in lime and sugar.
3. Pour over ice.
