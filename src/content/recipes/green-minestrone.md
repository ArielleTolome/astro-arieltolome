Add the greens at the end so they stay loud.

## Ingredients

- 1 can white beans
- 2 cups chopped greens
- 1/2 cup small pasta
- Garlic
- Parmesan rind or lemon

## Instructions

1. Sauté garlic, add beans and water, simmer.
2. Cook pasta in the pot.
3. Stir in greens 2 minutes.
