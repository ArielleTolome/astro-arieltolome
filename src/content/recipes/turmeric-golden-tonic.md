Black pepper is not optional; it wakes the turmeric up.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups oat milk
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 tsp honey
- Pinch black pepper

## Instructions

1. Warm oat milk with turmeric, ginger, and pepper. Do not boil.
2. Sweeten with honey.
3. Pour into mugs.
