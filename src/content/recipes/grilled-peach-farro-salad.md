Grill the peaches cut-side down until they stripe.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup farro, cooked
- 3 peaches, halved
- Arugula
- 1 tbsp vinegar
- Olive oil

## Instructions

1. Grill peaches 3 minutes.
2. Toss farro with oil, vinegar, salt.
3. Add arugula and sliced peaches.
