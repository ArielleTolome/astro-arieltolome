Salt, wait, drain. Then chili. Otherwise it waters down.

## Ingredients

- 2 cucumbers
- 1 tsp chili oil
- Flaky salt
- Vinegar splash

## Instructions

1. Cut spears, salt 5 minutes, pat dry.
2. Toss with chili oil and vinegar.
