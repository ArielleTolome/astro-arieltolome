Roast until the edges almost burn. That’s the sweetness.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb carrots
- 1 onion
- 1 inch ginger
- 1 can coconut milk
- Broth

## Instructions

1. Roast carrots and onion at 425°F for 30 minutes.
2. Blend with ginger, coconut milk, broth.
3. Salt.
