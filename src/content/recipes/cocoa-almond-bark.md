Toast the almonds. Raw ones taste like wood.

## Ingredients

- 8 oz dark chocolate
- 1 cup almonds, toasted
- Flaky salt

## Instructions

1. Melt chocolate. Stir in most almonds.
2. Spread on parchment, top with remaining almonds and salt.
3. Set in the fridge.
