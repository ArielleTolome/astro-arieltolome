It should look underbaked in the middle. It isn’t.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- 3 eggs
- 3/4 cup olive oil
- 1 orange, zested and juiced
- 1 1/2 cups flour

## Instructions

1. Whisk sugar, eggs, oil, orange.
2. Fold in flour and a pinch of salt.
3. Bake 350°F about 40 minutes.
