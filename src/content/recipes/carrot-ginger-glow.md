Fresh carrot, not juice from a bottle, or it tastes thin.

## Ingredients

- 3 carrots, chopped
- 1 orange
- 1 inch ginger
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- Water

## Instructions

1. Blend with a splash of water until it moves.
2. Strain if you hate pulp. Don’t if you don’t.
