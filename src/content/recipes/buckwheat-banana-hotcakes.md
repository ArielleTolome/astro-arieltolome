Buckwheat browns fast. Medium heat, not high.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup buckwheat flour
- 1 cup oat milk
- 1 egg or flax egg
- 2 bananas
- Butter or oil

## Instructions

1. Whisk flour, milk, and egg. Rest 5 minutes.
2. Cook small cakes until bubbles set.
3. Fry banana slices in the same pan and stack them on top.
