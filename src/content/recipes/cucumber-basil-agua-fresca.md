Strain it or it tastes like salad water. The basil goes in at the end.

## Ingredients

- 2 cucumbers, peeled
- 1 lime, juiced
- 8 basil leaves
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 3 cups cold water

## Instructions

1. Blend cucumber, water, sugar, and lime.
2. Strain through a fine mesh.
3. Tear in basil and chill.
