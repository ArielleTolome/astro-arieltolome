Shave the fennel paper-thin or it tastes like raw onion.

## Ingredients

- 2 fennel bulbs
- 2 oranges
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Fennel fronds

## Instructions

1. Shave fennel on a mandoline.
2. Segment oranges over the bowl so juice falls in.
3. Oil, salt, fronds.
