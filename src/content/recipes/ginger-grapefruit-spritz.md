Not a cocktail unless you want it to be. The ginger syrup keeps in the fridge for a week.

## Ingredients

- 1 ruby grapefruit, juiced
- 1 inch fresh ginger, sliced
- 2 tsp honey
- 1 cup sparkling water
- Ice

## Instructions

1. Simmer ginger with honey and 3 tbsp water for 5 minutes. Cool.
2. Strain the syrup into two glasses of ice.
3. Add grapefruit juice, top with soda, stir once.
