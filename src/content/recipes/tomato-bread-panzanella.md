Salt the tomatoes first and use that juice as dressing.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb ripe tomatoes
- 4 cups toasted bread cubes
- Basil
- Red wine vinegar
- Olive oil

## Instructions

1. Salt chopped tomatoes 10 minutes. Save the juice.
2. Toss juice with oil and vinegar.
3. Add bread and basil. Rest 10 minutes.
