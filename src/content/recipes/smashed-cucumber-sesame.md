Smash, don’t slice. You want ragged edges that hold sauce.

## Ingredients

- 4 cucumbers
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- Chili crisp

## Instructions

1. Smash cucumbers under a knife, tear into chunks, salt 10 minutes.
2. Drain. Toss with garlic, vinegar, sesame.
3. Chili crisp on top.
